## Limitations encountered

- The VITA architecture and corpus are proprietary in the primary PDF; the paper points to a companion preprint, but that source was not supplied and was not imported.
- The source cites public Figshare datasets and OpenAI simple-evals; these were not part of the task source set and were not used to verify reproducibility.
- The primary leaderboard reports no confidence intervals; only the sensitivity mean-per-question scores include 95% CIs.
- The paper does not provide a controlled corpus-ablation experiment, so corpus specificity remains a hypothesis rather than an isolated causal mechanism.
- The current LiteratureAtom schema is oriented to primary biomedical literature rather than multi-model benchmark matrices; generic exposure/outcome/result fields were used without changing the schema.
