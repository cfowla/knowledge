## Focused triage findings

### Judge-competence diagnostic

The paper derives a necessary chance-floor condition for reference-free judge gating: under its latent-solver model, judge discriminability can exceed chance only if grading-context competence clears `1/k`, where `k` is answer-space size. It also shows that marginal ROC-AUC is confounded by shared item difficulty, motivating a within-question estimator. For Sonnet, within-question AUC is 0.489 on research mathematics and 0.735 on factual QA; GPQA-Diamond has only a marginal AUC of 0.735 because repeated within-question strata are unavailable.

### Benchmark-accuracy inflation

On GPQA-Diamond, Sonnet's answered-only accuracy is 0.840, falling to 0.626 when non-convergent episodes remain in the denominator, 0.547 on a less-exposed proxy, and 0.602 by AUC inversion. The authors attribute the largest 21.4-point gap to censoring convention and a further 7.9-point residual to possible benchmark exposure, while acknowledging proxy mismatch.

### Closed-loop failure analysis

In the small pilot, judges that fail the screen have false-accept rates of 0.33 on factual QA and 0.50 on mathematics. The passing factual-QA judge has false-accept rate 0.00 and false-reject rate 0.44, suggesting the screen may distinguish harmful acceptance from conservative over-rejection. The study explicitly does not establish sustained safety under drift or Goodhart pressure.

## Extraction limitations / schema gaps

- LiteratureAtom has no AI/ML-specific benchmark-result, judge-evaluation, or gate-safety atom kinds; these findings use existing quantitative/qualitative/method/limitation kinds plus descriptive tags.
- The latent-solver theory is intentionally scoped to single-answer reference-free judging and does not cover rubric- or evidence-grounded verification.
- GPQA-Diamond has no within-question strata; its AUC results therefore retain the marginal difficulty confound.
- The four-judge cross-provider matrix also reports marginal AUC because per-episode scores were not retained.
- The less-exposed proxy may differ from GPQA-Diamond for reasons other than benchmark exposure.
- Closed-loop evidence uses only 2-3 seeds and is adequate for directional/error-type claims, not stable effect-size estimates.
- Long-run selection pressure, drift, and Goodhart effects are not tested.
