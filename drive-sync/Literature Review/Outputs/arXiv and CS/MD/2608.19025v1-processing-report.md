## Citation and extraction findings

- Fixed-corpus extraction looked strong with an expert prompt, but context fields remained weaker than numerical fields.
- Same-model repetition exposed random and systematic error. A single run was not a stable estimate.
- Autonomous literature discovery was the sharpest failure point. The best mean reference recall was 53.7%.
- Manual citation verification mattered. Qwen3.7-Plus hallucinated 27 of 60 unique citations. GLM-5.2 hallucinated 20 of 69.
- Consensus strength provided a usable review trigger. Agreement with human consensus fell from 93.2% for unanimous 5-0 calls to 55.2% for 3-2 splits.
- Five calls spread across different models reached 93.3% agreement with reviewer consensus. Five same-model calls reached 89.2%.

## Validation and limits

- Every atom uses one publication UUID and a source page, figure, or table anchor.
