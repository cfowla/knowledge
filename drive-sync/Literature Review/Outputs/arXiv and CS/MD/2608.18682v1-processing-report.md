## Focused triage findings

### Optimization formulation

RTPO replaces flattened multi-turn training with a turn-boundary formulation. It preserves the exact rollout conditioning context, optimizes turns in reverse order, forks same-state sibling continuations for turn-level advantages, and regenerates downstream continuation under the current policy. The method therefore targets rollout–training mismatch, cross-state credit contamination, and asynchronous downstream drift with separate but coupled mechanisms.

### Trajectory comparisons

The eight-benchmark main table reports 61.66 overall for RTPO versus 53.71 for GRPO and 57.68 for SeeUPO, with reported relative improvements of 21.50% and 10.76%. Controlled credit-assignment experiments favor RTPO-CA, while on-policy continuation improves GAIA, WebWalkerQA, and XBench but is essentially tied on HLE. Supplementary τ3-Airline evaluation adds a genuinely stateful tool environment and compute-matched comparisons, but also shows a ~41.3% training-cost increase and late-stage performance regression.
