## @SEA appraisal notes

The strongest evidence is on structured skill and software-engineering tasks. Qwen3.5-9B + SKILLER scores 73.91 on SkillsBench and 82.80 on SWE-Skills-Bench, versus the strongest listed non-SKILLER baselines of 60.87 and 62.40. Results are not uniformly superior across all cells: Qwen3.5-9B ties SkillX on GAIA; Qwen3.5-4B trails SkillX on SkillsBench and GAIA; and Qwen3.5-4B trails Manus on the held-out EarthBench split. The HTML therefore qualifies the paper's broader “consistently outperforms” language.

A reproducibility ambiguity is retained in appraisal: the main experimental setup states a five-step SKILLER schedule across tasks, while Appendix E describes the released SkillsBench configuration as three optimization rounds. This was not silently reconciled.
