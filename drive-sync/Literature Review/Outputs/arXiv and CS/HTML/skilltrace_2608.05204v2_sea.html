One-page synthesis

Core thesis. Reuse of mixed-modality LLM-agent skills can survive in different substrates even when whole-package similarity collapses. SkillTrace therefore audits three provenance traces separately - Expression, Implementation, and Operational - and surfaces a pair when any trace exceeds a same-function-negative-calibrated threshold.

The most distinctive contribution is the Operational Trace, represented as a typed Skill Operational Graph (SOG) whose Activation, Procedure, and Resource-Flow views abstract host-facing behavior. An LLM is used once at ingestion to produce the SOG; pairwise auditing thereafter is deterministic over cached traces. Expression and Implementation use normalized token overlap, while Operational similarity combines block, activation, procedure, and resource-flow matching.

On SKILLTRACE-BENCH, built from 100 marketplace anchors, 820 transformed reuse positives, and 751 negative controls, MaxFusion reports AUROC 0.938, precision 0.914, recall 0.883, and F1 0.898. Its strongest advantage appears under transformed reuse: for full LLM rewrites (P3), repository Jaccard detects 32% while Operational detects 73% and MaxFusion 78%. Partial-lift families illustrate why trace attribution matters: implementation-lift and documentation-reuse cases fire through different evidence channels.

The 36,446-skill deployment audit is useful as a feasibility/triage study rather than prevalence evidence. Among 204,386 exact-scored candidate pairs, 43,581 were jointly flagged by repository and static SkillTrace evidence; 3,030 additional pairs were SkillTrace-static-only. The paper also reports practical amortization: about 26 seconds and $0.01 for one-time Operational extraction per skill, then about 105 ms per cached pair with no audit-time token cost.

Main caution. The benchmark is partly transformation-generated, uncertainty estimates are not reported, and the paper does not clearly state that threshold calibration uses a held-out N1 subset. Table III also shows that MaxFusion's system-level detection rate on N1 strict negatives is 0.10 even though each individual trace threshold is calibrated to the 95th percentile (nominal 0.05 per trace). These are important constraints on interpreting the headline AUROC/F1 as deployment-ready error rates.

Section-by-section condensation

Abstract & I. Introduction

The paper frames agent skills as mixed-modality packages whose reusable value can reside in prose, code fragments, activation rules, tool-use procedures, or resource flow. This creates a provenance problem different from conventional code-clone detection: a derivative can preserve a high-value script or workflow while rewriting enough surrounding material that whole-package similarity falls, while independently built same-function skills can collide on generic templates and APIs.

SkillTrace's response is trace-attributed auditing rather than a single resemblance score. It separates Expression, Implementation, and Operational evidence; the latter is intended to function as a skill-level operational birthmark. The stated contributions are the problem formulation, multi-trace framework/SOG, SKILLTRACE-BENCH, and a large public marketplace audit.

II. Background

The authors position SkillTrace between classical clone detection, software birthmarks, repository-level fuzzy similarity, and prompt-protection work. MOSS/JPlag/CCFinder/SourcererCC-like token methods motivate static reuse signals; control/data-dependence and software-birthmark ideas motivate the structural Operational Trace.

The skill-ecosystem discussion emphasizes that agent artifacts contain more than prompts or code and are distributed through registries/marketplaces. The closest baseline is a repository-level Jaccard/ssdeep approach, instantiated as RepoClone. Prompt watermarking and prompt-stealing literature is treated as complementary because SkillTrace assumes already-published packages and performs post-hoc provenance review.

III. SkillTrace Design

A reuse candidate is defined as a candidate preserving at least one concrete provenance trace beyond similarity expected from same-function independent skills. Expression tokens authored natural-language substrates; Implementation tokens executable/command substrates and is marked not applicable when code/command material is insufficient. Operational Trace uses a SOG with normalized operational blocks described by action, object role, tool role, inputs, and outputs.

Operational similarity combines block matching (0.30), activation overlap (0.10), procedure similarity (0.40), and resource-flow similarity (0.20). Each trace has a threshold set at the 95th percentile of N1 strict-negative scores. The final SkillTrace score is the maximum normalized trace score; beta=1.0 surfaces a pair when any applicable trace meets its threshold. Reports retain the firing trace and matched evidence pointers to support human review.

IV. Evaluation - benchmark construction & setup

SKILLTRACE-BENCH uses 100 public marketplace anchors and 820 positive reuse pairs spanning repackaging (R1-R2), port/rewrite (P1-P3), and partial lift/replacement (L1-L3). Positives combine observable real reuse with controlled transformations. The 751 negatives include N1 same-function strict negatives mined from the real corpus and N2 clean-room skills generated from task metadata without access to the original body. N1 calibrates thresholds; N2 is a boundary control.

Labels are filtered using cross-model judging and then manually checked. Baselines are RepoClone-Jaccard and RepoClone-ssdeep. Controlled evaluation reports AUROC, precision, recall, F1, per-family scores, and detection rates; the wild-corpus study reports review-queue findings rather than supervised metrics.

IV-D. RQ1 - detection effectiveness

MaxFusion reaches AUROC 0.938, precision 0.914, recall 0.883, and F1 0.898 against the combined N1+N2 negative pool, compared with RepoClone-Max AUROC 0.841 and F1 0.783. The hardest transformation regime is porting/rewriting. For P3 full LLM rewrite, RepoClone-Jaccard mean similarity is 0.156 with detection rate 0.32; Operational is 0.681 with rate 0.73; MaxFusion fires on 0.78.

Partial-lift results demonstrate trace rotation: L1 implementation lift yields Expression 0.160/0.42 detection but Implementation 0.835/1.00 and Operational 0.936/1.00; L3 documentation reuse favors Expression (0.468/0.88) and MaxFusion reaches 0.98 detection. R-class near-literal reuse saturates all methods.

IV-E. RQ2 - trace complementarity

Family-wise correlations support complementarity rather than independence. For partial lifts, Expression-Implementation correlation falls to 0.15; for port/rewrite, Expression-Operational and Implementation-Operational are 0.28 and 0.36. The authors report roughly one hundred Expression-only positives, roughly seventy Operational-only positives, and only a few Implementation-only positives at beta=1.0.

Equal linear fusion is precision-oriented (0.973) but has recall 0.744 and F1 0.843; MaxFusion keeps lower precision (0.914) but materially higher recall (0.883) and F1 (0.898). This matches the forensic design choice: one localized trace may be sufficient to justify routing a candidate to review.

IV-F. RQ3 - ecosystem discovery

The wild audit covers 36,446 SkillHub/ClawHub skills and is explicitly not a prevalence estimate. In the top-50 anchor scan, 49/50 hot anchors have at least one jointly flagged candidate and 357 joint candidates are surfaced; 18 additional SkillTrace-only candidates appear across 8 anchors. Software-engineering skills contribute 211 candidates across 30 anchors.

For global routing, candidate generation uses repository, Expression, and Implementation indexes; Operational is deliberately excluded because generic operational schemas can collide. Of 204,386 exact-scored candidate pairs, 43,581 (21.3%) are jointly flagged, 3,030 (1.5%) are static-SkillTrace-only (1,529 Expression-only; 1,338 Implementation-only; 163 both static traces), and 439 (0.2%) are RepoClone-only. A blinded stratified review of 200 pairs (100 flagged, 100 low-priority controls) is described qualitatively as supporting inspectable trace-aligned evidence.

IV-G/H. RQ4 - cost, scalability & threats to validity

Cold Operational extraction is reported at about 26 seconds and $0.01 per skill (approximately 10K input + 1K output tokens). For 36,446 skills this is about $360, 263 single-worker hours, or 5.3 hours with 50 workers. Cached full MaxFusion scoring averages 105 ms/pair; 204,386 candidate pairs correspond to about 6 single-worker hours or 22 minutes with 16 workers, with no audit-time token cost.

Stated threats include LLM-generated positive transformations, incomplete market coverage, possible SOG noise/coarseness, code-light Implementation inapplicability, generic operational collisions, shared templates/API wrappers/multi-origin merges, and detector-aware evasion not represented in the benchmark. The authors appropriately frame outputs as review candidates, not adjudications.

V. Discussion & VI. Conclusion

The discussion emphasizes registry use cases: duplicate management, dispute review, quality ranking, linking maintained variants, and warning about smaller/outdated derivatives. The trace label is central to actionability because it indicates whether evidence is textual, executable, or operational.

The legal boundary is explicit: SkillTrace does not determine protectability or infringement. Operational similarity is also not treated as sufficient by itself for generic skills. The paper concludes that trace-attributed auditing improves transformed-reuse detection over package-level baselines and suggests future use of these traces for skill quality assessment and trace-aware search.

Algorithm 1 - SkillTrace audit procedure

Input: reference skill R, candidate skill C
Load thresholds (tau_E, tau_I, tau_O) and decision bound beta (default 1.0)
Extract/cache traces T_R and T_C = (Expression, Implementation, Operational)
For each applicable trace k:
    s_k = TraceSimilarity(T_R[k], T_C[k])
    z_k = s_k / tau_k
z = max(z_E, z_I, z_O)
Collect evidence for traces with z_k >= beta
If z >= beta: return SURFACED(z, evidence)
Else:         return NOT_SURFACED(z, evidence)

Governance: Output is triage evidence for human review, not a legal verdict.

Evaluation & appraisal

Verdict: Read first. The paper is highly relevant and methodologically concrete for agent-skill provenance, reliability, and registry governance. Its operational-birthmark concept and separation of one-time semantic extraction from deterministic audit-time scoring are build-useful. Treat the reported performance as promising benchmark evidence rather than a settled deployment error rate.
Best useArchitecture reference for provenance-aware skill registries; benchmark design ideas; trace-attributed audit queues; deterministic post-ingestion comparison.
Do not use forLegal infringement determinations, intent/ownership claims, marketplace-wide prevalence, or guaranteed detection against adversarially detector-aware reuse.

Ratings

Relevance9/10

Rationale: Directly addresses reusable agent skills, provenance, auditability, deterministic evaluation, and marketplace governance.

Evidence basis: Full framework + benchmark + wild-corpus deployment study.

Limiting factor: Narrow focus on provenance reuse rather than broader runtime correctness/security.

Would raise/lower: Broader integration with runtime trust or real registry operations would raise it; if skill provenance is not a target workflow, relevance falls sharply.

Novelty8/10

Rationale: Multi-trace skill provenance and the SOG operational birthmark are a meaningful adaptation of clone/birthmark ideas to mixed-modality agent skills.

Evidence basis: Three-trace design, SOG views, trace-attributed evidence, MaxFusion.

Limiting factor: Token overlap, calibrated thresholds, and structural birthmarks each have established predecessors.

Would raise/lower: Demonstrating uniquely necessary SOG information beyond simpler structured task graphs would raise confidence in novelty.

Method strength7/10

Rationale: Uses strict same-function negatives, controlled transformation families, baselines, single-trace/linear-fusion ablations, and a deployment audit.

Evidence basis: 820 positives, 751 controls, R/P/L families, Tables III-V, 36,446-skill corpus.

Limiting factor: Some positives are LLM-generated; family denominators and uncertainty intervals are not reported; a held-out threshold-calibration split is not described.

Would raise/lower: Pre-registered held-out calibration/test splits, confidence intervals, external benchmark replication, and adversarial evasions would raise it.

Evidence strength6/10

Rationale: Results are internally coherent and show the intended trace-complementarity pattern, but evidence remains benchmark-centric and partly synthetic.

Evidence basis: AUROC/F1 gains, transformation-family behavior, wild review queues, sampled manual inspection.

Limiting factor: No reported uncertainty; wild validation is qualitative; no independent replication; calibration/evaluation separation is unclear.

Would raise/lower: Independent labeled datasets and transparent error-analysis counts would raise it; detector-aware attacks showing rapid degradation would lower it.

Evidence basis: SkillHub/ClawHub deployment study and top-50/global audits.

Limiting factor: Public registries, model-generated transformations, and unknown transfer to other host formats or adversarial users.

Implementation value8/10

Rationale: The paper gives a clear boundary between ingestion and audit, explicit scoring equations, trace applicability rules, cost estimates, and review evidence outputs.

Evidence basis: Algorithm 1, equations 1-5, fixed Operational weights, calibration rule, cost/scalability study.

Limiting factor: The paper does not enumerate the full closed SOG vocabularies, extractor prompt/schema, or an end-to-end public implementation in the provided text.

Would raise/lower: Released schemas/prompts/code and reproducible benchmark fixtures would make it directly implementable.

Critical appraisal notes

  • System-level false-positive calibration is higher than 5%. The authors correctly describe 5% per-trace calibration. Because MaxFusion fires when any trace crosses threshold, Table III shows 10% on N1 strict negatives and 12% on N2. Deployment review-budget claims should therefore be based on fused-system behavior, not per-trace tau alone.
  • Calibration split is not clearly reported. The paper says thresholds are calibrated on N1 and then reports N1 performance; it does not state in the provided text that N1 was split into independent calibration and evaluation subsets. If the same N1 observations informed tau and the reported negative performance, those error rates are not fully out-of-sample.
  • Repo-ssdeep threshold ambiguity. Table III reports a threshold of 0.000 for Repo-ssdeep while RepoClone-Max is described as using calibrated component scores. The handling of a zero normalization threshold is not explained in the paper text, which is a small but concrete reproducibility gap.
  • Wild manual validation is directionally useful but under-quantified. The 200-pair blinded review is described with qualitative terms rather than a confusion matrix, reviewer count/agreement, or explicit adjudication criteria.
  • Operational Trace is both the key novelty and the highest-risk abstraction. Its value depends on the information content and stability of the LLM-extracted SOG; the paper acknowledges that generic tasks may converge on common graphs and therefore excludes Operational evidence from global routing.

Implementation / research takeaways

1. Separate ingestion from auditIf semantic normalization needs an LLM, pay that cost once, version the representation, cache it, and keep recurring pairwise decisions deterministic.
2. Keep provenance channels separateDo not average away a strong localized signal. Preserve trace-specific scores and evidence so reviewers know whether prose, implementation, or operational structure fired.
3. Calibrate against hard same-function negativesGeneric task similarity is the core confounder. Strict negatives are more informative than random unrelated negatives for provenance systems.
4. Report fused-system error ratesPer-channel calibration does not guarantee the same false-positive rate after an OR/max decision. Measure review-queue burden after fusion.
5. Treat operational similarity as corroboration when schemas are genericThe paper's own global routing excludes Operational Trace because common workflows can collide. Information content should influence how strongly an operational match is trusted.
6. Preserve review evidence, not just scoresMatched text/code tokens and aligned SOG blocks/edges are central to defensible human review and debugging.
High-value next experiment: Reproduce the method with an explicitly held-out calibration set, publish the SOG schema/vocabularies and extractor prompt, add detector-aware transformations, and report system-level precision/recall with confidence intervals plus detailed human-review agreement.
