## Extraction limitations

- The retrieved PDF contains the 8-page main article. Supplemental Figures 1-3 and Supplemental Table 1 are referenced in the article but are not embedded in the retrieved PDF, so their detailed data were not atomized.
- Sample set 1 contains 39 anonymized patient samples, but the number of unique patients is not reported in the main article; atoms therefore describe sample-level rather than participant-level denominators where applicable.
- Several statements about projected UFH dose changes are classification-based consequences, not observed administered-dose or clinical-outcome comparisons.
- The prolonged-UFH/antithrombin and body-weight relationships are exploratory observations from a very small patient cohort; the atoms preserve them as reported without upgrading them to causal findings.
- The study did not measure clinical bleeding or thrombotic outcomes across the alternative monitoring strategies.
