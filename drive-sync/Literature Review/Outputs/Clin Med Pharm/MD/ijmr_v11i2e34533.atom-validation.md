## Extraction limitations

- The evaluated PDF contains the main article but only links/descriptions for Multimedia Appendices 1-4; appendix contents were not present in the retrieved PDF and were not atomized.
- The paper reports 2158 records after UFH/aPTT blending for model development, while Table 2 and the multiclassification confusion matrix use 2783 records. These reported populations were preserved as written rather than silently reconciled.
- Regression performance in Table 5 is reported without a table-specific denominator; regression-performance atoms therefore use a labeled model-development population without inventing a sample size.
- The article predicts aPTT response after prespecified doses; it does not validate an algorithm that selects the UFH dose itself.
- Model-extracted atoms are marked `needs_review`; no human verification status was fabricated.
