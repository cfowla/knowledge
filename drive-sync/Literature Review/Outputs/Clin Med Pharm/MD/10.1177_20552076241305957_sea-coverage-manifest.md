## Appraisal constraints identified before scoring

- Single-center retrospective source.
- 3,790 intervals from 211 patients / 286 ICU stays create repeated-measures structure.
- Train/test allocation was performed at the interval level. The paper reports 279 ICU stays represented in training and 224 in testing despite only 286 unique included stays; arithmetic therefore requires at least 217 stays to appear in both sets. This is a calculated source-derived finding and creates a major independence/leakage concern for performance estimates.
- No prospective or external validation.
- Clinical thrombotic and hemorrhagic outcomes were unavailable.
- The best-performing approach depends heavily on prior anti-Xa and therefore does not solve initial UFH dosing.
- Supra-therapeutic prediction was the weakest class.
- No model-calibration method was applied.
