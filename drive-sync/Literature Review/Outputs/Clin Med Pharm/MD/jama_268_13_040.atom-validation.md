## Source-type handling

The LiteratureAtom schema is designed for primary literature, while this source is a task-force report that summarizes other studies and issues recommendations. To avoid falsely implying that the task force itself generated the cited trial data:

- task-force recommendations and interpretive statements are represented mainly as `author_conclusion` atoms;
- task-force process statements are represented as `method` atoms;
- numerically reported findings from cited studies are retained as `quantitative_result` atoms and tagged `secondary_reported_result`;
- no bibliography entry was treated as evidence by itself;
- no missing trial details (arm denominators, confidence intervals, effect estimates, etc.) were invented;
- no appraisal judgment was converted into reported evidence.
