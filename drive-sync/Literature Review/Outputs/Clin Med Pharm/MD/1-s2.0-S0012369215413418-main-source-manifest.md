## Coverage decision

- All substantive narrative pages 456S-477S were inspected.
- All 11 main-text tables are reconciled in the SEA artifact.
- Bibliography-only pages 478S-481S are retained as provenance infrastructure and omitted from atomization/routine condensation.
- The paper is a secondary review/consensus source. Trial-level quantitative atoms are explicitly tagged as secondary reported results; they do not imply that Cairns et al. enrolled the reported study populations.
- Table 7 contains at least one row whose displayed therapy/control percentages and reported relative-risk-reduction direction are difficult to reconcile. Those ambiguous row values are discussed in SEA but were not promoted into quantitative atoms.

- The source does not report a transparent systematic search/selection method comparable with a modern systematic review.
- Current-practice alignment is not asserted; this is a 1992 historical source and requires modern guideline verification before clinical implementation.
