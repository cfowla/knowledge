## Extraction limitations

- The paper is a secondary association analysis within a randomized parent trial; achieved aPTT categories were not randomized.
- The study measured approximately 18-hour patency rather than directly observing reocclusion.
- The complete serial-aPTT analysis included 94 patients, including 16 with a closed infarct-related artery.
- Day-7 angiography was available in 60 selected patients who had an initially patent artery and returned for protocol angiography.
- The source is historical (1992). Current guideline/practice alignment was not externally verified in this run.
