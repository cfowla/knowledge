## Interpretation boundary

The confirmatory evidence supports the primary cardiovascular-death-or-HF-hospitalization composite, total first/recurrent HF hospitalizations, and eGFR slope. The primary effect is chiefly hospitalization-related: cardiovascular death was HR 0.91 (95% CI 0.76-1.09), and all-cause mortality was HR 1.00 (0.87-1.15). Outcomes outside the hierarchy are retained as nonconfirmatory. The trial is empagliflozin versus placebo and is not a direct empagliflozin-versus-dapagliflozin comparison or proof of therapeutic interchangeability.
