## Source-numbering note

The source's Limitations paragraph cites reference 20 for immortal-time bias. In the source bibliography, reference 19 is the immortal-time-bias article by Yadav and Lewis, while reference 20 is the Juurlink et al. hyperkalemia article. The list above preserves the source's numbering rather than silently changing it.
