# Cannata and McDonagh 2025 HFpEF reference task queue

Source: Cannata A, McDonagh TA. Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction. N Engl J Med 2025;392:173-184. DOI 10.1056/NEJMcp2305181.

Purpose: preserve the article bibliography in source order and create follow-up tasks for primary-source ATOM and SEA processing. The source is a narrative Clinical Practice review, so cited trial results in the review remain secondary reports until the cited publications are processed directly.

Priority key: P0 = direct diagnostic guidance, pivotal HFpEF treatment evidence, or major current guideline; P1 = important supporting evidence; P2 = background or contextual evidence.

All 61 printed references were extracted. Bibliographic details were preserved as printed. No external bibliographic correction was applied.
