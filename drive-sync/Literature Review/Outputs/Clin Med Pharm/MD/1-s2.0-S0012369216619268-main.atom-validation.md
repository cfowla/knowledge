## Provenance and secondary-source handling

The governing LiteratureAtom model is oriented toward primary literature, while this source is a consensus review. The extraction therefore preserves source type explicitly:

- numerical trial outcomes summarized in the article are tagged `secondary_reported_result` and anchored to this review rather than represented as outcomes generated by Hyers et al.;
- graded practice statements are represented as `author_conclusion` atoms and tagged `guideline_recommendation` (plus `grade_A` or `grade_C` when explicitly reported);
- no bibliography entries were atomized as evidence;
- no missing sample sizes, confidence intervals, recommendation grades, or study details were invented;
- source-language uncertainty and conflicts (for example, abbreviated heparin duration and thrombolytic long-term benefit) were retained rather than reconciled externally.
