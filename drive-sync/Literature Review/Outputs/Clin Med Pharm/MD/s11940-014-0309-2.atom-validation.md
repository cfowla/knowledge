## Secondary-source handling

The governing LiteratureAtom model is designed for primary literature, while this source explicitly states that the authors performed no human or animal study. Extraction therefore uses a secondary-source guardrail: claims about cited trials, meta-analyses, guidelines, or observed rates are represented as statements **reported by this review** and tagged `secondary_reported_result` where applicable. They are not represented as if Marshall and Connors generated those primary data.

The current schema has no dedicated `review_summary`, `guideline_recommendation`, or `secondary_reported_result` atom kind. Recommendations are therefore represented as `author_conclusion`; secondary evidence summaries are represented as `qualitative_result`, `limitation`, or `other` with descriptive tags. This is a schema limitation, not an extraction failure.

## Extraction limitations

- This is a narrative review/opinion article with no explicit systematic search strategy, risk-of-bias framework, or reproducible evidence-selection method.
- Bibliography pages were not atomized; cited primary studies should be extracted separately when trial-level atoms are required.
