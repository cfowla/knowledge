## Source inconsistencies preserved

- Participant-flow accounting is unclear: the text reports 359 reviewed and exclusion categories totaling 221, which would leave 138, while 131 are reported as included. The source does not reconcile the difference.
- Major bleeding across total-body-weight categories is reported with p=0.94 in Table 4 and p=0.9362 in the Discussion, but the Results narrative reports p=0.26 for the same 5/37, 3/65, and 2/29 counts.
- Table 4 reports major bleeding by BMI category as 0/43, 2/58, and 1/30 (3 total), which does not reconcile with the overall 10 major bleeding events reported in the same table. This was preserved as a source inconsistency rather than repaired.
