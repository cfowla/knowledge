## Key interpretation boundary

This source demonstrates persistent but attenuating cardiorenal benefit after randomized empagliflozin exposure and supports long-term SGLT2 treatment in eligible CKD. It does **not** directly compare empagliflozin with dapagliflozin and should not be used alone as evidence of molecule-level interchangeability.
