## ATOM scope decision

The governing LiteratureAtom model is explicitly designed for primary literature, while this source is a systematic review. The extraction therefore records **review-level assertions and pooled secondary results** only. It does not relabel individual trial findings as if this review enrolled participants or generated those trial data. Primary-study atoms would require separate extraction from the cited primary publications.

## Extraction limitations

- The source is secondary literature; the current LiteratureAtom schema has no dedicated systematic-review or guideline atom kind.
- Trial-level dosing regimens and endpoint definitions varied. The extraction does not normalize these into a single regimen where the source does not support one.
- Follow-up timing varied substantially across trials and outcomes; pooled review results are therefore anchored as “over all reported time periods” rather than assigned a single time horizon.
- Subgroup results are retained only where the review reported them. Unstable-angina-versus-NSTEMI subgroup analysis was not possible.
- The review itself rates the clinically important evidence as low quality and notes limited applicability to later dual-antiplatelet practice.
