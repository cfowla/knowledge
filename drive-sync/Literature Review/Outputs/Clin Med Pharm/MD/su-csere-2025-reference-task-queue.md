## Queue notes

- References 21–37 are the 17 unique RCTs represented in the review and are the preferred primary-literature extraction targets.
- Review-level atoms from Su et al. do not substitute for primary-study atoms from these RCTs.
- Canagliflozin ranking claims deserve particular primary-source scrutiny because the review states that the ranking relied on only two canagliflozin RCTs with 1,543 dual-diagnosis patients.
- Guidelines and observational comparative-effectiveness papers can inform implementation context but should remain distinct from the RCT evidence base.
