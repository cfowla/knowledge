## Source-level issues flagged for appraisal
1. The methods state that GLP-1 initiators were to remain event-free to the landmark for time-to-event analysis, while the Outcomes section also states that patients with outcomes before the landmark were included in the primary analysis. The relationship between those analytic populations is not fully explicit.
2. The 30-day abdominal-pain section reports median times to first coded event of 1,985 and 1,857 days, which exceed a strict five-year (1,825-day) horizon despite the stated follow-up of “up to 5 years.”
3. GLP-1 exposure pools five agents (including tirzepatide) without agent-specific dosing, persistence, adherence, or indication analyses in the reported main text.
