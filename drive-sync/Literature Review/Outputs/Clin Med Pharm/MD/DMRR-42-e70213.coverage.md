## Source consistency findings requiring preservation

1. **Table 3 fat-mass confidence interval:** For the 5%-10% weight-loss stratum, Table 3 reports fat-mass change of -5.41% with 95% CI -14.79 to **+3.97** and p=0.019, whereas the Results narrative on page 3 gives 95% CI -14.79 to **-3.97** with the same p=0.019. The table value is preserved in the table-anchored atom; the disagreement is flagged rather than silently corrected.
2. **Correlation statistic label:** The Results narrative describes weight-loss correlations as R²=0.38 for lean mass and R²=0.68 for fat mass, whereas Figure 1 labels these as Spearman **R=0.38** and **R=0.68**. Figure-anchored atoms preserve the Figure 1 notation.
3. **Abstract lean-mass correlation:** The abstract reports R²=0.36 (p<0.001) for the association of lean-mass loss with body-weight loss, while Figure 1 shows Spearman R=0.38 (p=0.0013). This is retained as an internal reporting discrepancy.

## Extraction/appraisal boundaries

- Treatment groups were pooled for the primary body-composition analyses; the article does not support comparative efficacy claims among liraglutide, semaglutide, and tirzepatide.
- The observational design and lack of an untreated/comparator group prevent causal attribution.
- Safety capture was limited to severe hypoglycaemia and diabetic ketoacidosis in the reported results/limitations; gastrointestinal and other adverse events were not captured.
- Total-body BMD findings do not establish site-specific hip or spine BMD stability.
