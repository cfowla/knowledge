# Reference task queue: Long-Term Effects of Empagliflozin in Patients with Chronic Kidney Disease

Source: EMPA-KIDNEY Collaborative Group. N Engl J Med. 2025;392:777-787. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2409183.

This queue preserves the article bibliography as provenance. Primary EMPA-KIDNEY publications and close CKD SGLT2 trials are prioritized for direct reading; references are not treated as evidence atoms merely because they are cited by this article.

Priority key: P0 direct EMPA-KIDNEY evidence needed to interpret this publication; P1 close class/guideline/design evidence; P2 landmark or mechanistic context; P3 background and methods.
