## Extraction limitations

1. Exact injection doses and pharmacokinetic assay methodology are not reported in the research letter.
2. The article does not report the numerical HIV-1 RNA assay detection limit or resistance-testing results.
3. Figure 1 shows individual paired concentration values graphically, but exact individual values are unlabeled; the extraction preserves the text-reported medians and ranges rather than estimating graph coordinates.
4. Proposed therapeutic targets and apparent half-lives are secondary literature context cited by the authors, not results generated by this case series.
5. The narrative calls 61–393 weeks a range, whereas the Figure 1 caption describes displayed summaries as median [interquartile range]; the text reports a median 17-week delay while the caption reports 17.4 weeks. These were retained as reporting ambiguities rather than silently reconciled.
