## Extraction limitations and source-level cautions

- The study is retrospective and the source explicitly notes hidden bias risk.
- Only 226 of 996 initially screened hospitalized AF/AFL patients entered the primary analysis; Figure 1 exclusions were preserved as separate patient-flow atoms.
- The study was not powered for age- or gender-based subgroup analyses.
- The source states that the high bleeding rate may have been partly related to concomitant warfarin plus IV UFH bridging.
- The cohort was from two northeastern U.S. hospitals and was predominantly White, limiting generalizability.
- Table 2 prints the creatinine threshold as **>2.6 g/dL**, while Table 1 reports creatinine in **mg/dL**. The atom preserves the Table 2 unit exactly as printed and tags it as a source-level unit inconsistency rather than correcting it.
- The reported >11 U/kg/h bleeding threshold was identified within the same dataset used for multivariable analysis; it was not prospectively validated in this paper.
- Zero inpatient strokes or thromboembolic complications should not be interpreted as proof of efficacy because events were rare and follow-up was limited to hospitalization.
