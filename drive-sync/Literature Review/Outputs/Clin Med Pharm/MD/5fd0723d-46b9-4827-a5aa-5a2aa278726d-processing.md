## Source details and preserved uncertainties

2. The main PDF states that model training used 5-fold cross-validation but does not clearly state whether the patient-level SHAP matrix used for clustering was generated strictly out-of-fold. The SEA flags this as a reproducibility/internal-validity uncertainty rather than assuming either implementation.
3. Cluster-specific O/E readmission patterns are within-cohort descriptive findings. Because clusters were created from SHAP attributions to the same readmission classes, the SEA does not treat those O/E associations as independent external validation.
4. Discussion statements about anticoagulation resumption, MMAE, infection monitoring/prophylaxis, and tailored care are author hypotheses/implications; they were not converted into tested treatment effects.
