## Source-content QA findings

1. **Patient-flow inconsistency:** Figure 1 reports 1,256 evaluated and 412 included; Results prose reports 1,255 evaluated and 411 included. The listed groups (317 prenomogram + 95 postnomogram) sum to 412, and Figure 1 also reports 844 excluded.
2. **Critical aPTT inconsistency:** the prenomogram percentage with aPTT ≥120 seconds is printed as 49.9% in the abstract, 49.2% in Results prose, and 49.3% in Table 2. The postnomogram value (46.8%), OR (0.92), and P value (.73) are consistent across the main result presentations.
3. **24-hour endpoint P-value inconsistency:** the abstract and Table 2 report P=.002 for therapeutic anticoagulation within 24 hours, while Results prose reports P<.0005.
4. These discrepancies were **not silently repaired**. They are preserved in the atom set with source anchors/tags and are explicitly identified in the SEA artifact.

## Extraction limitations

- The retrieved PDF is 6 pages and contains selectable text plus three figures and two tables; visual rendering was used to reconcile all pages.
- Supplementary material was not embedded in the PDF, so the exact dose-adjustment rows of the nomograms and the supplemental time-to-goal analysis could not be extracted.
- Several outcomes are laboratory surrogate/process measures. The article does not provide a controlled comparative patient-centered bleeding or recurrent-thrombosis endpoint sufficient to establish net clinical benefit.
- The article is internally inconsistent in a few reported counts/percentages/P values, as detailed above.
- No external guideline, label, or current-practice verification was performed because the requested workflow was source-grounded @ATOM + @SEA rather than @VERIFY.
