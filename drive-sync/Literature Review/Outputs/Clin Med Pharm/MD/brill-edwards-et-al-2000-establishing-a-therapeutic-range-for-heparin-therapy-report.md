### ATOM extraction limitations / source issues

1. **Filename-year mismatch:** the Drive filename contains `2000`, but the evaluated article identifies itself as a 1993 Annals of Internal Medicine publication. The article metadata was used for source identity while the original filename was preserved as provenance.
2. **Lot 102 year inconsistency:** Methods/Results describe the Dade lot 102 sample as **1988**, whereas Table 1 labels lot 102 as **1989**. Table-derived atoms preserve the Table 1 label; methods-derived atoms preserve the methods year.
3. **Ortho numeric inconsistency:** the Discussion prints the fixed-ratio Ortho heparin range as **0.09 to 0.018 U/mL**, while Table 2 reports **0.09-0.18 U/mL**. Table-derived atoms use the Table 2 value and the inconsistency is explicitly flagged rather than silently corrected.
4. **Range representation:** the current `QuantitativeResult` model requires a point estimate. Reported therapeutic ranges were therefore preserved as numeric canonical statements in `qualitative_result` atoms rather than converted to invented midpoints.
5. **Unreported fields:** no conflict-of-interest or data-availability statement was identified in the paper; none was invented.
6. **Secondary evidence in Discussion:** prior animal and clinical studies cited to justify the 0.2-0.4 U/mL reference band were treated as cited background/justification, not as primary results of this paper.
