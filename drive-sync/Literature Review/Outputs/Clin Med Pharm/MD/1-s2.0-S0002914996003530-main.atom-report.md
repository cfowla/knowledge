## Extraction boundaries and interpretation

The parent TIMI-5 trial randomized antithrombotic treatment, but the principal claims in this paper are serial biomarker-outcome associations. Atoms therefore do not treat biomarker levels as randomized exposures. Treatment assignments are represented as interventions/comparators where the article reports dosing or treatment-group comparisons; biomarker prognostic associations are represented as observational exposures.

Bivariate associations that report direction and p values without an effect estimate are represented as `qualitative_result` atoms rather than inventing an effect size. Table I means and Table II odds ratios are represented as `quantitative_result`/`adverse_event` atoms with structured numerical context.

## Extraction limitations

1. Marker-specific denominators varied because of specimen quality and missing measurements; the article reports that only about 50% of patients had valid values for each marker at all four time points.
2. Several figure-based associations provide p values and group sample counts but not directly tabulated effect estimates, limiting quantitative normalization.
3. The article performed many comparisons without multiplicity adjustment; those findings remain source-reported exploratory associations rather than confirmed predictive effects.
4. Treatment heterogeneity (heparin plus four hirudin dose groups) and pooled outcome analyses complicate interpretation of treatment-specific biomarker behavior.
5. Funding, formal conflict-of-interest disclosure, and data-availability statements were not identified in the evaluated article pages and were not fabricated as atoms.
