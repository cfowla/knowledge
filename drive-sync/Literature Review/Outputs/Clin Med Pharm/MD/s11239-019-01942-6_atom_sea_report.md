## Extraction / source limitations

1. The study is retrospective and single-center, and TBW-versus-ABW exposure was not randomized.
2. Baseline groups differed materially in body weight/BMI, age, and concomitant warfarin use.
3. Only 42/131 patients received the protocol-recommended initial bolus; protocol adherence was therefore heterogeneous.
4. Patient-flow arithmetic is internally incomplete: the listed exclusions total 221 of 359 reviewed, but 131—not 138—were analyzed.
5. The p value for major bleeding across TBW categories conflicts across source locations: Results narrative `p=0.26`, Table 4 `p=0.94`, Discussion `p=0.9362`.
6. The study reports laboratory target attainment rather than a powered comparison of thrombotic clinical outcomes, and safety event counts are small.
7. No external/current-practice verification was added; current guideline alignment requires a separate verification task.
