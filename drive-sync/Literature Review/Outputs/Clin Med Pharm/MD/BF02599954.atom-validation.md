## Internal source inconsistency

The abstract and main prose define **adequate heparinization (AH)** as the first PTT **>1.5 times** laboratory control. The footnote to Table 1 instead states AH is **>2.5 times** control. The abstract's reported AH rates (46% at 24 hours and 62% at 48 hours) match the Table 1 AH column, making the footnote definition internally discordant. Extraction preserves the abstract/prose definition and separately records the inconsistency rather than silently rewriting the source.

## Extraction limitations

- The study is retrospective and single-center, and its core anticoagulation outcome is a laboratory surrogate (PTT attainment) rather than a randomized clinical endpoint comparison.
- Historical cohort and nomogram comparisons are not concurrent randomized comparators; related atoms are explicitly tagged as secondary reports from the target article.
- The three post-heparin high-probability ventilation-perfusion scans are retained as a qualitative clinical signal, not converted into a causal adverse-event claim.
- The source reflects 1990-1991 IV UFH practice and should not be treated as a contemporary VTE-treatment standard without separate current-practice appraisal.
