### Source-content warnings preserved during extraction

- **comorbidity_summary_conflicts_table1:** The Results narrative states there was no significant difference in comorbid conditions, but Table 1 reports malignancy in 7.5% of ACA vs 24.1% of CDT patients with P=0.017. Table 1 is preserved as reported and the contradiction is flagged rather than reconciled.
- **age_p_value_internal_discrepancy:** The Results narrative reports P=0.894 for mean age, whereas Table 1 reports P=0.953. The age atom follows Table 1 and preserves this discrepancy here.
- **overall_removal_rounding_discrepancy:** The abstract reports CDT overall thrombus removal as 87.0%, whereas Results reports 87.1% (74/85). The atom uses the explicit fraction and Results value 87.1%.
- **calf_removal_order_internal_discrepancy:** The prose reverses the calf-vein group order in one sentence; Table 3 clearly reports ACA 36/38 (94.7%) and CDT 9/9 (100.0%). The atom follows Table 3.
