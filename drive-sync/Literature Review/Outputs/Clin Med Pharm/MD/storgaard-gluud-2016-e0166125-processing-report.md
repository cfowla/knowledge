Verdict: **Skim deeply**. This review is useful historical class-level evidence for glycaemic efficacy, body-weight/blood-pressure effects, and genital/urinary infection risk, but it predates most of the later cardiovascular, heart-failure, and kidney-outcome evidence and should not be used alone for a current SGLT2 formulary interchangeability decision.
