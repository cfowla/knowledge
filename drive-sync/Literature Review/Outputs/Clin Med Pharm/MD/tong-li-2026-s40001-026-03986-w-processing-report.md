## Source-integrity findings

- **Abstract / Table 1, BMI ≥35 kg/m²:** The reported GLP-1RA vs SGLT-2i point estimate (−1.11 kg) lies outside the stated 95% CrI (−6.73 to −4.60), so the interval cannot be mathematically valid as printed. Preserved exactly as reported; not corrected.
- **Results p3 vs Discussion p8:** Results reports 8 GLP-1 receptor agonists and 9 SGLT-2 inhibitors, while the strengths paragraph reports 7 GLP-1 receptor agonists and 8 SGLT-2 inhibitors. Results count retained as the main study-characteristics statement; discrepancy flagged.
- **Discussion p8 vs Results/Figure 4:** Discussion attributes weight MD −1.96 kg and LDL-c −0.24 mmol/L to GLP-1 receptor agonists; the main results report GLP-1RA weight MD −3.03 kg and LDL-c −0.14 mmol/L versus placebo, while −1.96 kg is the SGLT-2i weight estimate. No silent correction; appraisal flags the contradiction.
- **Table 1 note:** The printed direction-of-effect note (MD<0 favors column; MD>0 favors row) appears inconsistent with the narrative interpretation of the GLP-1RA vs SGLT-2i BMI subgroup estimates. Table values preserved; directional interpretation treated cautiously.
- **Abstract vs Figure 4:** GLP-1RA triglyceride 95% CrI lower bound is −0.22 mmol/L in the abstract but approximately −0.21 mmol/L in Figure 4. Figure 4 value used for the figure-derived atom; abstract discrepancy flagged.
- **Supplement Appendices 11–12:** Appendix/table numbering is internally inconsistent (e.g., Appendix 11 labeled “Appendix 1 1,” sensitivity table labeled S9.1, frequentist table labeled S10.1 despite another S10.1 in Appendix 10). Coverage uses semantic appendix labels rather than trusting duplicate table numbers.
- **Supplement sensitivity table:** HbA1c sensitivity values are on a body-weight-like scale and do not match the primary HbA1c estimates, consistent with a likely transcription/copy-paste error. These malformed values were not normalized into atoms.
- **Supplement frequentist NMA table:** Several entries contain malformed or internally impossible estimate/interval formatting. Frequentist validation is retained only as the source’s qualitative sensitivity claim; malformed cells are not converted into quantitative atoms.
