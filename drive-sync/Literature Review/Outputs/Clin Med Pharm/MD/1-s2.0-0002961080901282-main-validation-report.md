## Extraction boundaries and limitations

- Extraction used the original five-page PDF, with page rendering used to verify layout and Table I values.
- The source reports **121 unique patients but 156 DVT episodes/cases**. Several analyses, especially bleeding analyses, use case-level denominators totaling 156.
- Treatment-response analyses were restricted to objectively confirmed DVT; objective diagnostic support was reported for 71% of cases.
- Table I uses 59 female and 97 male cases, whereas the unique-patient cohort description reports 46 women and 75 men. The atom set preserves these source-specific denominator contexts rather than reconciling them silently.
- The article reports unusual dosing units for a “loading dose of 50 units/kg/hour” and a planned-study “bolus of 300 units/kg/hour.” These are preserved as printed and tagged `source_units_preserved`.
- Platelet counts were infrequently monitored; thrombocytopenia observations are therefore extracted qualitatively rather than converted into a reliable incidence estimate.
- Non-author meeting discussant comments on page 840 were not atomized as primary-study evidence. The SEA artifact summarizes them separately.
- No missing study detail was invented, and appraisal judgments were kept out of directly reported evidence atoms.
