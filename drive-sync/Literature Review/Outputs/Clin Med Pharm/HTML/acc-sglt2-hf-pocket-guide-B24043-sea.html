One-page synthesis

Core thesis: SGLT2 inhibitors are positioned as a central component of contemporary heart-failure therapy across the EF spectrum. The guide combines a stage-based GDMT map, HFrEF and HFpEF treatment algorithms, agent-level dosing, key contraindications/cautions, and medication-adherence tactics. For HFrEF, the guide depicts the four core therapies as ARNI, an evidence-based beta blocker, a mineralocorticoid antagonist, and an SGLT inhibitor. For HFmrEF/HFpEF, it gives SGLT2 inhibitors a Class 2a position under the 2022 guideline framework.

Highest implementation value: Table 1 condenses HF-specific SGLT2i use into 10 mg daily dapagliflozin/empagliflozin, 200→400 mg daily sotagliflozin, an eGFR ≥25 mL/min/1.73 m² initiation threshold for dapagliflozin/sotagliflozin, perioperative interruption, volume-depletion/diuretic considerations, ketoacidosis recognition, AKI/illness holds, infection warnings, and Fournier-gangrene precautions. Table 2 adds practical adherence interventions spanning pre-discharge initiation, cost/access, pharmacist involvement, teach-back, refill synchronization, and monitoring.

Currency limitation: the guide relies on the 2023 ACC HFpEF ECDP. ACC published a revised HFpEF ECDP on July 23, 2026 (doi:10.1016/j.jacc.2026.06.018). The guide's SGLT2i emphasis remains directionally aligned, but the full HFpEF treatment algorithm is no longer the current ACC consensus framework.

Section condensation

Pocket Guide Overview

The guide states that it is a streamlined implementation resource drawing on the 2024 ACC HFrEF ECDP, 2023 ACC HFpEF ECDP, and 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA HF guideline. It does not reproduce the development methods, evidence tables, or full recommendation rationale from those documents.

GDMT across heart-failure stages

The stage map places strong prevention therapies in Stage A/B and divides symptomatic/advanced HF by EF phenotype. HFrEF receives Class 1 ARNI/ACEi/ARB framing, beta blocker, MRA, SGLT2i, diuretic-as-needed, and hydralazine-nitrate for the specified African-American NYHA III-IV population. HFmrEF and HFpEF give SGLT2i Class 2a, with ACEi/ARB/ARNI, MRA, beta blocker, or ARB shown at weaker classes depending on phenotype.

HFrEF algorithm

The algorithm centers the four core HFrEF drug classes, then adds phenotype/problem-specific therapy: diuretic titration for persistent congestion, hydralazine/isosorbide dinitrate for persistently symptomatic African-American patients with NYHA III-IV disease, ivabradine for qualifying sinus-rhythm tachycardia, and vericiguat for high-risk worsening HF despite optimal GDMT.

HFpEF algorithm

The 2023-derived algorithm begins with SGLT2i and adds loop diuretic for fluid retention, MRA and ARNI in sex/EF-defined groups, and ARB when ARNI is otherwise appropriate but unaffordable or not tolerated. This is the section most affected by the 2026 HFpEF ECDP update.

Key considerations for SGLT2i

The guide states SGLT2i use across HF with or without diabetes and NYHA II-IV symptoms, on background GDMT. It lists dapagliflozin and empagliflozin at 10 mg PO daily and sotagliflozin at 200 mg daily initially with a 400 mg daily target. For dapagliflozin/sotagliflozin, it uses an eGFR 25 mL/min/1.73 m² initiation threshold and says a post-initiation fall below that level does not automatically require discontinuation.

Safety content covers type 1 diabetes/DKA risk, hypersensitivity, pregnancy, genital mycotic infection, volume depletion and possible diuretic adjustment, perioperative interruption for at least 3 days, euglycemic ketoacidosis assessment, temporary interruption during reduced intake or fluid loss, UTI/urosepsis/pyelonephritis, and rare Fournier gangrene.

Adherence and implementation

The ten adherence considerations are unusually practical for a short guide. They emphasize initiation during high-opportunity transitions, patient goals and learning preferences, regimen simplification, cost/access, clinician communication, pharmacist-supported education, real-time reminder tools, behavioral support, anticipatory counseling, refill synchronization, and direct adherence monitoring.

ECDPs, apps, and resources

The guide explicitly frames ECDPs as actionable consensus tools for areas in which evidence may be new, evolving, limited, or insufficient. It points to TreatHF and ACC resources as implementation aids rather than substitutes for the source guideline or full ECDP.

Figure 2 — HFrEF GDMT Treatment Algorithm

CORE: ARNI + evidence-based beta blocker + mineralocorticoid antagonist + SGLT inhibitor.
IF persistent volume overload / NYHA II-IV → titrate diuretic.
IF persistently symptomatic African-American / NYHA III-IV despite core regimen → add hydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate.

IF high-risk worsening HF despite optimal GDMT with HF hospitalization or IV diuretic requirement → add vericiguat.
FOOTNOTE: ACEi/ARB reserved for ARNI contraindication, intolerance, or inaccessibility; evidence-based beta blockers named are carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, bisoprolol.

Clinical validity and practice translation

Clinical question: How should SGLT2 inhibitors be positioned, prescribed, monitored, and implemented across heart-failure phenotypes? Validity: appropriate as a society-produced secondary implementation resource, not as evidence of treatment effect. Practice translation: strong for operational reminders, but the prescriber must reconcile renal function, volume status, diabetes/DKA risk, surgery/fasting, infection symptoms, current drug labeling, and newer HFpEF policy.

Currency verification. A subsequent 2024 ACC hospitalized-HF ECDP emphasized SGLT inhibitor therapy throughout hospitalization regardless of LVEF and supported inpatient initiation/continuation after stabilization (JACC 2024;84:1241-1267; doi:10.1016/j.jacc.2024.06.002). In July 2026, ACC published a revised HFpEF ECDP (doi:10.1016/j.jacc.2026.06.018), motivated by new SGLT2i, nonsteroidal MRA, and incretin-based evidence. These later documents do not invalidate the pocket guide's core SGLT2i message, but they do limit its value as a complete current HFpEF algorithm.

Implementation / practice takeaways

Useful now
Use as a compact checklist for SGLT2i dosing, perioperative interruption, volume status, DKA recognition, illness/AKI holds, infection counseling, and adherence supports.
Verify before policy use
Check current drug labels, renal thresholds, 2026 HFpEF ECDP, local formulary restrictions, and inpatient initiation protocol.
Formulary relevance
The guide presents dapagliflozin and empagliflozin at the same 10 mg once-daily HF dose, but it does not establish therapeutic interchangeability or equivalence between agents.
Do not overclaim
Class recommendations and benefit framing are secondary-policy summaries; they are not head-to-head evidence, and the guide does not report comparative efficacy between dapagliflozin and empagliflozin.
