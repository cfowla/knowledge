One-page synthesis

The infographic compresses the SGLT2i HF message into four elements: a benefit claim, HF dosing across EF phenotypes, renal-function footnotes, and a contraindication/caution matrix. It states that SGLT2 inhibitors reduce cardiovascular death or HF hospitalization regardless of type 2 diabetes, are used with background GDMT, and use 10 mg daily dosing for dapagliflozin/empagliflozin and 200→400 mg daily for sotagliflozin.

Its strongest value is rapid safety recall: type 1 diabetes/DKA risk, hypersensitivity, pregnancy, genital mycotic infection, volume depletion, perioperative interruption for at least three days, metabolic-acidosis assessment for ketoacidosis regardless of glucose, illness/fluid-loss interruption, UTI/urosepsis/pyelonephritis, and Fournier gangrene. It also explicitly says HF and diabetes use have different therapy considerations.

Source ambiguity: the HFmrEF dose row visually reads “LVEF ≤41-49%,” while the companion pocket guide defines HFmrEF as LVEF 41-49%. This is most plausibly a typesetting error, but the source itself is internally inconsistent and should not be silently normalized when used as a data source.

Visual / table extraction

Benefits callout

CLAIM: reduce cardiovascular death or hospitalization for HF regardless of presence/absence of type 2 diabetes.
LIMIT: no effect size, absolute risk, trial citation, time horizon, or component-specific estimate is shown.

Dosing matrix

POPULATION: Stage C & D HF with NYHA II-IV symptoms; HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF.
Dapagliflozin: 10 mg PO daily.
Empagliflozin: 10 mg PO daily.
Sotagliflozin: 200 mg PO daily starting; 400 mg PO daily target.
RENAL FOOTNOTE: for dapagliflozin or sotagliflozin, initiate at eGFR ≥25 mL/min/1.73 m²; later decline below threshold does not automatically require discontinuation.
EVIDENCE FOOTNOTE: “No RCTs for patients specifically with HFmrEF exist.”

Contraindications / cautions matrix

CONTRAINDICATIONS: type 1 diabetes (DKA risk); known hypersensitivity.
CAUTIONS: eGFR <25 for dapagliflozin/sotagliflozin in HF care; pregnancy; genital mycotic infections; volume depletion/possible diuretic adjustment; ketoacidosis precautions; ≥3-day pre-surgery interruption; metabolic-acidosis evaluation regardless of glucose; temporary interruption with reduced intake/fluid loss; urosepsis/pyelonephritis; Fournier gangrene.
SCOPE NOTE: therapy considerations differ for HF vs diabetes.

Practice translation

The infographic is appropriate as a reminder after the governing policy and drug label are understood. It is not sufficient evidence for therapeutic interchangeability, formulary equivalence, or a head-to-head comparison of dapagliflozin versus empagliflozin.

Currency verification: ACC published a revised HFpEF ECDP in July 2026. The newer document still identifies SGLT2 inhibitors as cornerstone HFpEF therapy but expands the HFpEF framework with later evidence and additional therapeutic classes. Therefore this 2024 infographic remains useful for SGLT2i safety recall but is not a complete current HFpEF treatment framework.
