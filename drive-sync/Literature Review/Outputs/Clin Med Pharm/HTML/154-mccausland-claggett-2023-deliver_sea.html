Dapagliflozin and Kidney Outcomes in Patients With Heart Failure With Mildly Reduced or Preserved Ejection Fraction

Prespecified DELIVER randomized-trial analysis — Summary, Evaluation, and Appraisal

Verdict: Read first. Strong for cardiovascular treatment-effect consistency across the enrolled kidney-function range and for the pattern of early eGFR decline followed by slower chronic decline. Weak for hard kidney-event benefit because events were sparse.

Synthesis

The analysis included 6262 DELIVER participants; mean age was 72 years, 56% were male, mean eGFR was 61 mL/min/1.73 m², and 49% had eGFR below 60. For cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure, dapagliflozin versus placebo produced HRs of 0.84 (95% CI 0.70–1.00), 0.68 (0.54–0.87), and 0.93 (0.76–1.14) across baseline eGFR strata ≥60, 45–<60, and <45, respectively. The eGFR-category interaction P value was .16 and the continuous-eGFR interaction P value was .45.

Dapagliflozin produced an expected acute eGFR decrease by month 1, with a between-group difference of −3.3 mL/min/1.73 m², then slowed chronic decline from month 1 onward by 1.4 mL/min/1.73 m²/year. The post hoc kidney composite occurred in 79/3131 dapagliflozin and 73/3131 placebo participants, HR 1.08 (95% CI 0.79–1.49). Thus, the eGFR trajectory is better supported than a hard kidney-event benefit in this analysis.

Methods and limitations

DELIVER enrolled adults aged at least 40 years with symptomatic heart failure, LVEF greater than 40%, elevated NT-proBNP, and structural heart disease; notable exclusions included eGFR below 25 mL/min/1.73 m² and type 1 diabetes. Participants were randomized 1:1 to dapagliflozin 10 mg daily or matching placebo. Cox interaction models assessed treatment effects across kidney-function strata. Repeated-measures mixed models estimated acute, chronic, and total eGFR slopes from measurements at baseline and months 1, 4, 12, 24, and 36.

The sustained kidney composite was too infrequent for meaningful interpretation, leading to use of a non-sustained post hoc definition. Median follow-up was 2.3 years. Urine albumin excretion was not measured. Analyses used a two-sided alpha of .05 without multiplicity correction. These points constrain claims based on kidney events and selected subgroup comparisons.

Evaluation and appraisal

Relevance 9/10
Direct fit for SGLT2, HFmrEF/HFpEF, and kidney-function questions.
Novelty 7/10
Useful kidney-function analysis of an established parent trial.
Method strength 8/10
Randomized parent trial, prespecified analyses, repeated renal measurements; multiplicity and post hoc kidney definition reduce certainty.
Evidence strength 8/10
Cardiovascular and eGFR-slope results are precise; hard kidney events are underpowered.
External validity 7/10
International cohort with substantial CKD representation but no eGFR below 25 and no albuminuria stratification.
Implementation value 9/10
Useful for interpreting treatment effect across moderate kidney dysfunction and the early-versus-chronic eGFR pattern.

Overall appraisal: cardiovascular interaction findings are credible within the enrolled eGFR range because treatment was randomized and the kidney-function analysis was prespecified. The chronic eGFR slope result is clinically interpretable. The analysis does not establish reduction in end-stage kidney disease or kidney death.
