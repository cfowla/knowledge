Accessible-source synthesis

The abstract reports evaluation of 15 LLMs on 145 publicly available BPS certification practice questions spanning 14 specialty domains. Questions were submitted with a standardized prompt without added prompt engineering, models were used in default user-facing settings, and answers were scored against BPS-posted answer keys. Accuracy was summarized overall and by specialty; between-model differences were tested with Cochran's Q and Bonferroni-adjusted McNemar comparisons.

Mean accuracy was 86.2% (SD 3.5%), averaging 125/145 correct answers. Perplexity AI was lowest at 79.3% (95% CI 72.6%-86.0%) and Microsoft Copilot (GPT-5) highest at 91.7% (95% CI 87.2%-96.3%). Overall model performance differed (Q=46.262; df=14; p<0.001). Microsoft Copilot (GPT-5), Gemini 2.5 Flash, and OpenAI o3 (Reasoning) outperformed Perplexity AI after adjustment; Copilot GPT-5 also outperformed Copilot GPT-4.1. Significant specialty-level differences were reported for Solid Organ Transplantation Pharmacy and Nuclear Pharmacy.

Preliminary implementation / research takeaways

  • The benchmark is directly relevant to pharmacy-focused LLM evaluation and supports comparison across specialty domains.
  • Reported accuracy is high, but the range across models and version-to-version change reinforces the need to identify the exact model/version and repeat validation over time.
  • Do not treat BPS practice-question accuracy as evidence of clinical safety or readiness for autonomous clinical decision support.
